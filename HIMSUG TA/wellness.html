Mental & Emotional Wellness

Recognize your feelings, make time to rest, and reach out to someone you trust when things feel overwhelming.

  • Talk with a trusted friend or support person.
  • Take short breaks between study sessions.
  • Ask for professional support when needed.
☀

Stress Management

Manage school demands by taking tasks one step at a time and building routines that include rest.

  • Break large tasks into smaller steps.
  • Plan realistic study and rest periods.
  • Try slow breathing or a short walk.
＋

Physical Health

Support your body with everyday habits that contribute to general well-being.

  • Drink water regularly.
  • Choose balanced meals when possible.
  • Include movement and adequate sleep in your routine.
☷

Healthy Study Habits

Healthy routines can make it easier to focus while avoiding unnecessary exhaustion.

  • Set manageable daily priorities.
  • Keep your study area comfortable.
  • Take screen and stretching breaks.
☻

Social Connection

Positive relationships and supportive communities can help students feel connected.

  • Stay connected with supportive people.
  • Join activities that interest you.
  • Offer kindness and listen to others.
✚

Know When to Seek Help

Do not hesitate to ask for help when a concern affects your daily activities or well-being.

  • Visit the school clinic for appropriate concerns.
  • Explore available guidance or counseling support.
  • Seek urgent help in an emergency.
