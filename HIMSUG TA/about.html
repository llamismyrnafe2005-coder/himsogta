KAABAG PRESENTS

Getting clinic care should feel simple and accessible.

HIMSOG TA! is a proposed student-centered school clinic information and appointment platform for the University of Science and Technology of Southern Philippines (USTP). It brings clinic information and appointment requests together in one convenient place for Trailblazers.

WHO WE ARE

About KAABAG

KAABAG is the proposing company behind HIMSOG TA!. Through this project, KAABAG aims to make it easier for USTP students to learn about school clinic services and understand how to access them.

Our concept is guided by the belief expressed in our tagline: “Atimanon ang panglawas, dili kinahanglan mahal.”

OUR PLATFORM

What is HIMSOG TA!?

HIMSOG TA! is designed as one platform where students can:

  • View clinic services and general requirements.
  • Learn how to access school clinic support.
  • Choose a preferred appointment schedule.
  • Send an appointment request for clinic confirmation.

OUR PURPOSE

Why HIMSOG TA! matters

Our presentation identified a need to make students more aware of the school clinic and the services available to them. HIMSOG TA! responds by making clinic information easier to find and appointment requests more convenient.

01 · Inform

Present clinic services and requirements in one easy-to-access platform.

02 · Connect

Help students select a preferred schedule and submit an appointment request for clinic confirmation.

03 · Support

Promote awareness of physical and mental wellness resources for the USTP community.

LISTENING TO TRAILBLAZERS

Built around what students shared.

The student survey presented in the KAABAG pitch showed that many respondents had not used the campus clinic. Students also identified lack of awareness and limited time as possible barriers to accessing services.

61.1%of respondents said they had never used campus clinic services.
38.9%of respondents said they had used campus clinic services.

Students also expressed interest in mental health counseling, stress management sessions, basic medical checkups, and wellness seminars.

THE ROAD AHEAD

From concept to campus access

As outlined in the KAABAG presentation, the project will move forward in stages and in coordination with USTP School Clinic personnel.

NOW

Ongoing student survey and proposed platform design.

NEXT

SOON

Develop and pilot HIMSOG TA! at USTP-CDO, subject to approval.

FUTURE

Expand to more USTP campuses and improve access to clinic services.

ⓘ

Project status: HIMSOG TA! is a proposed website/platform concept. It is not an official USTP clinic system. Clinic services, requirements, schedules, and appointment procedures must be reviewed and approved by the appropriate USTP personnel before launch.
